# What is Artificial Intelligence?

## Machines That Learn

Imagine teaching a child to recognize a cat. You don't hand them a rulebook that says "cats have pointed ears, whiskers, and a tail." Instead, you show them pictures — lots and lots of pictures. Eventually, they just *get it*. They can spot a cat they've never seen before.

That's basically what Artificial Intelligence (AI) does. It's software that learns from examples instead of following step-by-step instructions written by a programmer.

Traditional software works like a recipe: "If the user clicks this button, do that thing." AI works differently. You feed it thousands (or millions) of examples, and it figures out the patterns on its own.

## A Brief History (Without the Boring Parts)

The idea of AI has been around since the 1950s. Here's the quick version:

- **1950s** — Alan Turing asks "Can machines think?" and proposes the Turing Test
- **1960s-70s** — Early excitement. People predict human-level AI within 20 years (spoiler: they were wrong)
- **1980s-90s** — "AI Winter" — progress slows, funding dries up, hype fades
- **2000s** — More data + faster computers = things start working again
- **2012** — Deep learning explodes onto the scene. Image recognition suddenly gets *really good*
- **2022-present** — Large Language Models (like ChatGPT) change everything

The pattern? AI has always been "20 years away" — until suddenly it wasn't.

## The Three Types of AI

Not all AI is created equal. Think of it in three levels:

### 1. Narrow AI (What We Have Now)
AI that's really good at *one specific thing*. Your spam filter? Narrow AI. Netflix recommendations? Narrow AI. Siri, Alexa, Google Translate — all narrow AI.

It can beat the world champion at chess but can't make you a sandwich. It's a specialist, not a generalist.

### 2. General AI (The Goal)
AI that can do anything a human can do — reason, learn new things, understand context, be creative. We don't have this yet. Current AI is very clever at *appearing* general (like ChatGPT), but under the hood, it works very differently from a human brain.

### 3. Super AI (Science Fiction — For Now)
AI that surpasses human intelligence in every way. This is what movies like *Terminator* and *Ex Machina* are about. Most researchers agree this is decades away at minimum, if it's even possible.

## Why Should You Care?

AI isn't just a tech thing anymore. It's reshaping:

- **How we work** — writing, coding, design, customer service
- **How we learn** — personalized education, tutoring
- **How we create** — art, music, video generated from text
- **How we make decisions** — hiring, lending, medical diagnosis

Understanding AI isn't about becoming an engineer. It's about understanding the world you live in.

> *Think of it like electricity. You don't need to know how a power plant works. But you should understand what a light switch does.*

That's what this book is for — giving you the light switches.
